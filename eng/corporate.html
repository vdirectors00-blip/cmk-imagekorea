IMAGE PROGRAM

Corporate & Institutional Image Program

We have designed the integrated image capabilities needed in business into 9 modules. Target are employees and leaders interested in image-making; modules and content are customized by gender ratio, rank, and training objective.

Module.01

Integrated P.I. Strategy for Business People

The concept and importance of Personal Identity, self/external image diagnosis, and setting a target image (5 key points).

Module.02

Personal Color & Signature Color

Diagnosing and analyzing your signature color to add value to personal image and branding.

Module.03

Quick & Easy Business Makeup

Fast, easy hair and makeup know-how for a trustworthy impression.

Module.04

Image-Scale-Based Business Look

Styling the look and accessories that match your target image, based on body-type and bone-structure diagnosis.

Module.05

Business Manners That Raise Corporate Value

Organization-tailored training that builds a trustworthy image through situational manners.

Module.06

All-in-One Smart Mobile Card

From makeup, hair, and styling to profile photography — a personalized smart mobile business card.

Module.07

ICRU-Tool Disposition Color Diagnosis

Diagnosing 4 disposition types and stress factors and interpreting the results.

Module.08

ICRU True Self · Self-Management

Consulting self-management strategy based on inner P.I. and stress diagnosis.

Module.09

ICRU Leadership

Consulting leadership strategy based on inner P.I. and leadership diagnosis.

※ Modules 07–09 (ICRU) are in-depth diagnosis & analysis programs, conducted after prior consultation depending on topic and group size.
