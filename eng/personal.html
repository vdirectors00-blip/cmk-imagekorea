OVERVIEW

What is Executive Image Strategy?

Executive Image Strategy is CMK Image Korea’s private 1:1 program that diagnoses a leader’s impression, attitude, and language and designs them into a single strategy.

It uses a diagnostic system combining color theory, psychology, and non-verbal communication, aiming to align identity (the image you intend) with image (how others perceive you).

The program consists of Private 1:1 coaching, Media Image for broadcast, lectures, and official occasions, and Personal Style tailored to personal color, body type, and lifestyle.
